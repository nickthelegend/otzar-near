- Subjects: a jade treasury vault, stacked gold coins, a jade key, an hourglass (round expiry), a shield (guardians), a rising chart (yield), three guardian figures as jade tokens, a gold BTC-style coin without the ₿ mark.
- Files: `otzar-hero.webp`, stickers `otzar-st-{vault,coins,key,hourglass,shield,chart,guardians,btc}.webp`, `otzar-og.webp`, logo options `otzar-logo-a.webp` / `otzar-logo-b.webp`.
- Banned: real logos (no ₿ symbol, no exchange marks), people, readable text, letters.
