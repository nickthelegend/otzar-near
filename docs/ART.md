# Hako art direction
- Style: glowing wireframe-and-glass isometric objects, thin luminous ice-teal edge lines, frosted translucent faces, deep near-black void, subtle volumetric light, no clutter.
- Palette: #05090c void, #9fe7f5 ice, #5cc3db teal, #1f7f9b deep teal, a tiny #f2b84b amber accent only for warnings.
- Subjects: a sealed glass box (箱) vault with a key lattice inside, a bitcoin-like coin suspended inside a glass cube (no logo), a lattice/crystal key (post-quantum), a clock-dial lock (delay), a shield shard.
- Banned: real logos (no ₿ symbol, no exchange marks), people, readable text, letters.
